# Molecules

- `1grl.pdb`: PDB entry 1GRL, a subunit of the bacterial chaperonin GroEL (4,186 atoms: 547
  residues with their polar hydrogens; 19 helices and 11 sheet strands), as the Protein Data Bank
  distributed it in 1995 (its columns 73-80 hold the entry and a line number). cvc::mol's tests
  read it.

  Source: https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1GRL. Braig K, Otwinowski Z, Hegde R, Boisvert DC,
  Joachimiak A, Horwich AL, Sigler PB (1994), "The crystal structure of the bacterial chaperonin
  GroEL at 2.8 Å", Nature 371: 578-586. License: CC0 1.0 (the PDB archive's files are free of
  copyright restrictions); cite the structure's authors. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
